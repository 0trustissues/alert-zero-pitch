Before you go

Key takeaways

01

The SOC hits bottlenecks everywhere.

We covered two of them: blocking known threats at the endpoint, and hunting the unknown that never trips an alert. Both run on one platform, part of a bigger picture.

02

Prevention cuts the alert load at the source.

Every threat stopped before it executes is one fewer alert to chase and one fewer investigation to open.

03

Continuous hunting finds what prevention doesn't.

It runs continuously on your data and surfaces a finding only on a real hit. You see the query it ran and the evidence behind it, and you approve every action before anything ships.

This is AlertZero: agentic capabilities across Elastic Security that adopt at your pace, keep you in command of every consequential action, and run wherever your data lives.
